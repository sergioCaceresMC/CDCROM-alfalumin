# Fondo del cuaderno

`journal-table.png` se generó con la herramienta integrada **imagegen** como fondo decorativo. La interfaz, textos y controles se renderizan en HTML/CSS. La interfaz lo utiliza oscurecido detrás del cuaderno. `paper-grain.svg` aporta una textura ligera a los paneles y controles.

Prompt utilizado:

> Create one production website background illustration, landscape 1536x1024. Style: whimsical hand-drawn fantasy role-playing journal illustration, dark ink outlines, imperfect painted edges, matte watercolor and aged paper grain, vintage tabletop adventure board. A dark weathered wooden tabletop occupies the central two thirds with quiet uncluttered horizontal planks, no bright highlights, supporting readable parchment UI panels that will be composited separately in real HTML. Along the far left and right edges, illustrated mossy woodland leaves, small wildflowers, curling branches, scraps of faded hand-drawn fantasy map paper and tiny compass details frame the table. Hints of a dusky plum sky and a crescent moon in the upper left margin. Palette forest green, charcoal brown, faded plum, ochre and warm ivory. Flat storybook illustration, charming ink and watercolor craftsmanship, no pixel art, no neon, no photorealism, no text, no letters, no labels, no people, no UI panels, no buttons. Keep the center dark and low contrast; decorative details live mostly at the perimeter. This is a background asset only for an actual game companion website.
